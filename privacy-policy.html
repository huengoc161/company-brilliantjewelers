Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Brilliant Jewelers ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit brilliantjewelerstx.com (the "Site"), RSVP for our events, or contact us, and how we use and protect it.

Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you RSVP or fill out a form on the Site, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your preferred language and how you heard about us (optional)
  • Your consent to receive event reminders by text and email

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, we and our advertising and analytics partners may automatically collect information such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages you view, the website that referred you, and how you interact with the Site. This is collected through cookies, pixels and similar technologies.

Cookies, Pixels and Advertising

We use Google Tag Manager to load tools from Google, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok. These tools help us measure visits and RSVPs, understand which ads work, and show our ads to people who may be interested in Brilliant Jewelers, including people who have visited the Site before.

These partners may use cookies and similar technologies to collect information about your activity on the Site and on other websites. Their use of this information is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta: facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • TikTok: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also manage ad preferences in your Google, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok account settings, or opt out of interest-based ads at optout.aboutads.info.

How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm your RSVP and manage our events
  • To send you event reminders and updates by text and email, if you agreed to receive them
  • To answer your questions and provide customer service
  • To measure and improve the Site and our advertising
  • To keep the Site secure and prevent fraud
  • To comply with the law

Text Messages and Email

If you agree to receive messages from us, we may send you event reminders and updates. Message and data rates may apply. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP, and you can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email. We do not sell or share your mobile number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your name, phone number or email address. We only share personal information:

  • With service providers who help us run the Site, store RSVPs, and send messages, and only for those purposes
  • With advertising and analytics partners (Google, Meta and TikTok) as described above
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, our customers or the public
  • As part of a sale, merger or transfer of our business

How We Protect and Keep Your Information

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, or as required by law.

Your Choices and Rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you messages. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under your state's law. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Third Party Websites

The Site may link to other websites, such as our Facebook, Instagram and TikTok pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. Please review their privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
