New Era(03)

The line between what Sharpstown used to be and what comes next — starting now.

The comeback starts on purpose.

In 1961, Sharpstown Center opened as Houston's first air-conditioned, enclosed shopping mall. For a generation it was the city's premier destination — the new Houston, modern and ambitious.

The neighborhood never stopped moving. Today it's one of Houston's most diverse: Vietnamese, Chinese, Hispanic and Black families side by side, about 77,000 neighbors with a median age of 33, and fifteen years of fresh investment.

The reputation just hasn't caught up. Brilliant Jewelers isn't discovering Sharpstown — we're choosing it, as where the comeback starts.
